THE EXPERIENCE · EMIR

Extraordinary spaces.
An exclusive experience.

EMIR is an 82.5-metre ocean-going superyacht with a volume of 1,809 GT and accommodation for up to 28 guests in 14 cabins. Expansive interiors and generous entertainment decks offer comfort and privacy for large families, private groups and corporate events.

The Royal Master Suite features floor-to-ceiling windows, private balconies and a skylight above the king-size bed. An Executive Stateroom, two VIP suites and additional versatile guest cabins complete the accommodation.

Life on board includes a multifunctional spa, an outdoor gym on the upper deck and a dedicated cinema with an 8K Mini-LED screen. The sun deck's Jacuzzi and loungers invite relaxation, while outdoor lounge and dining areas provide generous space for socialising and meals overlooking the sea.

Stabilisers at anchor and underway, air conditioning and high-speed connectivity enhance comfort throughout each cruise. An onboard infirmary is equipped with a defibrillator and an electrocardiograph.

Things to know

📍Meeting point and boarding
EMIR’s departure port, berth and boarding time will be provided in the booking confirmation. Arrive 30 minutes early and confirm the meeting point with our team. Times refer to Dubai (UTC+4).
💳Payment and deposit

We accept Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, AMEX and JCB. A non-refundable 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking. The remaining 50% must be paid no later than 24 hours before departure.

❌Cancellation policy
Cancellation and amendment terms will be provided with your proposal and confirmed before payment. Contact our team for details.
👨‍✈️Captain and boating licence
EMIR has a professional crew. Guests do not need a boating licence.
⚓Onboard conduct and safety
Follow the captain and crew’s instructions. Supervise children and ask permission before swimming or using equipment.
