— Our history

Redefined luxury experiences on the waters of Dubai.

Oberoi Yachts was born with the aim of offering refined and unforgettable sailing experiences, combining luxury boats with exceptional hospitality. Our fleet of stylish yachts and high-performance motorboats is designed to ensure comfort, style and impeccable sailing experience along the beautiful coast of Dubai.

Whether to celebrate a special occasion, organize a corporate event, enjoy a sunset cruise or leave for a day of adventure, every trip is taken care of in detail. Thanks to a professional crew and a constant commitment to a high-level service, we focus on offering experiences that go far beyond a simple boat trip, turning every excursion into an unforgettable memory in the waters of Dubai.
